Team essentials
Uniforms
Uniform rules for the season
Home games: blue jersey + white pants + blue socks. Away games: white jersey + gray pants + blue socks. Practice: any practice jersey + athletic pants. No metal cleats — rubber or molded only.
Communication
How the team communicates
Coach Mike emails all updates to the team list. Last-minute changes come via text. This page updates automatically within minutes of any email or text going out — always check here first.
Weather policy
Game & practice cancellations
Games cancelled if lightning is within 8 miles or field is unplayable. Coach Mike will text by 3 PM for evening events. This page will show a red CANCELLED banner at the top if anything changes.
Snack schedule
Post-game snack rotation
Each family is responsible for one post-game snack for ~15 kids. The order follows the roster list top to bottom. The schedule tab will flag your week automatically.
Playoffs
Playoff format & eligibility
Top 6 teams advance. Single elimination starting May 29. Players must have appeared in at least 4 regular season games to be eligible. We're currently 2nd in the division.
Volunteering
Ways to help out
We need scorekeepers, field prep volunteers (arrive 30 min early), and a team photographer. Reply to any team email or talk to Coach Mike if you can help.
